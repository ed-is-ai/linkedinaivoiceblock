<objective>
Re-point the eval scoring loop from the raw `classifyPost` call to a selectable detector engine (`--engine heuristic|llm`, default `llm`), and add best-F1-threshold error analysis (FP/FN) to both the terminal output and the persisted results JSON.

Purpose: The Phase 26 eval bypassed the detector pipeline the extension actually ships, so its per-post `signalBreakdown` used the LLM's own signal vocabulary instead of the heuristic names stored in `flaggedAccounts.signals` (D-01, D-03). Scoring through `HeuristicDetector` makes metrics and signal breakdowns reflect deployed behavior. Error analysis (D-05) turns the results into something actionable by listing the misclassified posts at the chosen threshold.

Output: Modified `scripts/eval.ts` (engine flag, `buildPostData`/`filterErrors` exports, FP/FN section, extended results schema), expanded `scripts/eval.test.ts`, and an updated `eval-instructions.md`.
</objective>
