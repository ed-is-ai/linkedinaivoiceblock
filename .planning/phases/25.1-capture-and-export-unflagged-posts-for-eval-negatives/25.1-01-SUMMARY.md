## What Was Built

Three purely-additive declarations in `src/shared/types.ts`:

1. **`UnflaggedPost` interface** — modeled on `StoredPost` but with `seenAt` instead of `hiddenAt`, an `engineUsed: 'heuristic' | 'llm'` field (D-04), and an optional `label?: string` that the extension never writes (D-06/D-07). Placed directly after `StoredPost`.

2. **`StorageSchema.unflaggedPosts?: UnflaggedPost[]`** — sibling key beside `storedPosts`; doc-comments describe the 200-entry cap, oldest-evict policy, opt-in gate, and who reads/writes.

3. **`Settings.captureUnflaggedPosts?: boolean`** — opt-in field, OFF when undefined (D-05). Doc-comment calls out the broader privacy surface relative to flagged-post storage. New field with no prior toggle to reuse.
